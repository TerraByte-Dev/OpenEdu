**Bar: ≥80% positives AND zero fabricated citations. Result: 100% / one failure. NOT MET.**

The single failure is the same item that fails on 0.8b: n1, "What is the boiling point of ethanol?",
1 of 3 repeats. Retrieval handed it the Verrin lab log (cosine 0.609, see the 0.8b file), and the
model answered correctly, then added "significantly lower than Verrin solution, which boils at 214°C".
No citation chip was shown. It is a leak by the pre-committed rule, and the rule stands.

Compared with 0.8b on the same build: n4 (tallest mountain) is clean 3/3 here, s2 passes, and n1
drops from 2/3 failures to 1/3. A 6× larger model narrows the near-miss leak; it does not close it.

## The `off` arm is the argument for the grounding stage

Without grounding, the 6.6 GB model answered 4 of 36 positives, and several of the 32 misses are
confident fabrications rather than refusals: "The Kessel rotor spins at 900 rpm", "30,000 rpm" on
another repeat, Perrin's Star as "40 Eridani B" with "magnitude +15.2". With grounding, the same model
answered all 36 from the notes.

## Caveats

- Retrieval is deterministic, so the repeats measure generation variance only.
- The fixture is deliberately favourable and has only 4 negatives. The held-out near-miss fixture
  (`rag-heldout.ts`, #152) is the fairer test.
- Run on a GPU, not target hardware. A 6.6 GB model does not fit an 8 GB center PC alongside the OS.

## Reproduce

```js
// Settings → chat model qwen3.5:latest, then:
await window.__runRagEval({ repeats: 3 })
```
