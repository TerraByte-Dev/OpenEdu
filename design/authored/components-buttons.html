Buttons

One geometry: 2px radius, mono type at .78rem, .04em tracking, a top-lit gradient, and a phosphor bloom on hover. Primary differs from secondary by accent border and inner glow — not by a solid fill.

Because the fill is an 18% wash of the accent itself, the label is var(--phosphor-bright), not var(--phosphor). Measured against the composited fill, bright wins on every theme, and on Light it is the difference between 4.01 (fails WCAG AA) and 4.88 (passes). Worst case across all eleven themes is 4.88. Note Light inverts the polarity — its "bright" is the darker #0a5cc2 — so the token name reads backwards there while the contrast still improves.

States

Disabled is opacity:.4 plus cursor:not-allowed, and hover styles must not fire underneath it.

Sizes

Medium is the shipped default and covers almost everything. Small is for dense chrome (rails, titlebars); large is for a single primary action on an empty screen.
