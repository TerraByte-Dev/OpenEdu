Glow & Elevation

OpenEdu has no drop shadows. On a pure-black emissive surface a grey shadow is invisible at best and muddy at worst, so depth and emphasis are carried by phosphor bloom instead. This is the fastest way to tell a real OpenEdu surface from an imitation of one.

Elevation, such as it is

There is no z-scale of shadows. A surface is raised by being a lighter background tier with a hairline border; it is active by gaining a glow. Those are different axes and shouldn't be conflated.

flat — border only
active — border + glow
