Linked vs unlinked

Linked — a fact

Someone wrote [[Calvin cycle]]. The edge exists; it is in the graph. Shown expanded by default because it is real structure.

Unlinked — a suggestion

A note says "Calvin cycle" in prose. Might be worth linking, might be incidental. Collapsed by default, and matched conservatively — whole-word only, and titles under three characters are skipped entirely. A suggestion that fires constantly is worse than one that never fires.

Keep these separate. Merging them into one "mentions" count would state a relationship the vault doesn't actually have.
