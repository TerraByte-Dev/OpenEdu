Form Controls

Inputs are the one place OpenEdu commits hardest to the terminal: LCD face, accent text, a permanent inner wash, and a focus state that blooms rather than outlines. .cf-input ships in index.css and carries all of it.

Text input

Placeholder is --ink-faint; typed text is --phosphor with a soft text-shadow, which is what gives it the phosphor-screen look.

Focus is a border-colour change plus a double bloom — no outline ring, and no layout shift.

Long-form input switches to --font-body (Lexend). Prose the user writes should be as readable as prose the app writes.

Toggle

Ships as role="switch" with aria-checked — the one control in the app that gets its ARIA right. Keep that.

Segmented control

For 2–5 mutually exclusive options that fit on one line. More than that, or longer labels, and it becomes a select.

Markup

<input class="cf-input" placeholder="…">
<textarea class="cf-input"></textarea>
<select class="cf-input">…</select>
<button class="tg on" role="switch" aria-checked="true">…
