Space, Radius & Layering

The app has no spacing or radius tokens — every value is a literal. These three scales are derived from what actually ships, so they describe the product rather than reforming it. A generator needs concrete numbers; without them it invents a new scale on every run.

Derived from the shipped distribution — rounded-lg 87 uses, rounded-xl 48, rounded-full 42, plus the 2px and 4px hardcoded in the CSS. rounded-md (9) and rounded-2xl (5) are stragglers and are not part of the scale. The tight 2px on terminal chrome is deliberate: soft corners undercut the CRT read.

Nothing sits above the CRT layer except toasts, the titlebar, and boot. Scanlines cross modals — that is correct and intentional, because the overlay is the screen, not a decoration on top of the content. The app already obeys this by accident; the scale makes it a rule.
