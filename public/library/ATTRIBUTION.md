## Diagrams

Every SVG under `assets/` is generated by `scripts/build-assets.mjs` from curated data, and is
reproducible byte-for-byte by re-running it. **No diagram in this repository is AI-image-generated**,
by policy — see `AUTHORING.md`. Image models hallucinate element symbols, atomic masses and
capitals, which is the exact failure this layer exists to prevent.

## Source licence classes

Units record a `provenance` and a `source_url` per claim. These are the classes the build recognises,
in increasing order of restriction. `commercial_ok: false` is set by anything `-NC`.

| Class | SPDX / label | Bundle in the app binary? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public domain dedication | `CC0-1.0` | yes | First-party content. |
| US Government work | `US-Gov-PD` | yes | **Read the page, not the domain** — see the caution below. |
| Attribution | `CC-BY-4.0` | yes | Attribution line must render in the UI. |
| Attribution–ShareAlike | `CC-BY-SA-4.0` | **no — own pack only** | Viral onto close paraphrase. Quarantined. |
| Non-commercial | `CC-BY-NC-4.0` and variants | **no** | Sets `commercial_ok: false`, permanently. |

### Caution — a US Government domain is not a US Government licence

A federal domain can host third-party copyrighted material, and the most useful pages are often the
ones that do.

**MedlinePlus is the live example.** Health topic pages authored by the National Library of Medicine
are US Government works and are public domain. The **Medical Encyclopedia at
`medlineplus.gov/ency/*` is A.D.A.M., Inc. content — copyrighted and licensed to NLM, not public
domain.** It is also the most procedure-shaped, most directly copyable material on the site, which is
precisely why an automated pipeline would reach for it first.

The build enforces this rather than trusting policy:

- `provenance: us-gov-pd` on any `medlineplus.gov/ency/` URL → **build fails**.
- A claim with any non-original provenance and no `source_url` → **build fails**.
- Text implying NLM or NIH endorsement → flagged for human sign-off.

## Excluded sources, and why

Recorded so nobody re-proposes them.

| Source | Why it is excluded |
|---|---|
| **OpenStax** | Relicensed to CC BY-NC-SA in July 2026 **and** carries an explicit clause forbidding ingestion into large language models. The AI clause is independent of the NC clause and is independently disqualifying — it cannot be used as model input at all, in any tier. |
| **CK-12** | Moved off Creative Commons to a proprietary licence. |
| **Wikimedia projects** | CC BY-SA. Usable as research input; facts drawn from it are free. **Close paraphrase is an adaptation** and would make the derived unit share-alike, so no unit may cite it as `pd_source`. |
