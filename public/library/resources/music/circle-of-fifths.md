# Circle of Fifths

Moving **clockwise** adds sharps (up a perfect fifth each step); moving **counterclockwise** adds flats.

## Sharp keys (clockwise from C)
- C major — 0 sharps — relative minor: A minor
- G major — 1 sharp (F#) — E minor
- D major — 2 sharps — B minor
- A major — 3 sharps — F# minor
- E major — 4 sharps — C# minor
- B major — 5 sharps — G# minor
- F# major — 6 sharps — D# minor
- C# major — 7 sharps — A# minor

## Flat keys (counterclockwise from C)
- F major — 1 flat (Bb) — D minor
- Bb major — 2 flats — G minor
- Eb major — 3 flats — C minor
- Ab major — 4 flats — F minor
- Db major — 5 flats — Bb minor
- Gb major — 6 flats — Eb minor
- Cb major — 7 flats — Ab minor

## Order and mnemonics
- Order of sharps: **F C G D A E B** — "Father Charles Goes Down And Ends Battle."
- Order of flats: **B E A D G C F** — the reverse ("BEAD" then G C F).
- Find a sharp key: take the last sharp and go up a half step (e.g. last sharp F# means key of G).
- Find a flat key: it is the second-to-last flat (e.g. flats Bb Eb Ab means key of Eb).
- Relative minor: a minor third (3 half steps) below the major key; shares the same key signature.
