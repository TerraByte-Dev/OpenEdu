# Major & Minor Scale Patterns

A scale is built by a fixed pattern of **whole steps (W)** and **half steps (H)**. (W = 2 half steps; H = 1.)

## Major scale
Pattern: **W–W–H–W–W–W–H**
- C major (no sharps/flats): **C D E F G A B C** — the half steps fall E–F and B–C.

## Minor scales (three forms)
- **Natural minor:** **W–H–W–W–H–W–W**
  - A minor: **A B C D E F G A**.
- **Harmonic minor:** natural minor with the **7th raised** a half step (A: …F **G♯** A) — creates the leading tone.
- **Melodic minor:** **6th and 7th raised** going **up**, natural (lowered again) coming **down**.

## Key relationships
- **Relative minor** = the 6th degree of a major scale; shares the same key signature (C major ↔ A minor).
- **Parallel minor** = same starting note, different key (C major ↔ C minor).
- Each major key and its relative minor are a **minor third (3 half steps)** apart.
