# The Grand Staff & Clefs

Music is written on a **staff** of 5 lines and 4 spaces. The **grand staff** joins the treble and bass staves with a brace, linked by **middle C** on a ledger line between them.

## Treble clef (𝄞 G-clef — higher pitches; right hand)
- **Lines** (bottom→top): **E G B D F** — "Every Good Boy Does Fine."
- **Spaces** (bottom→top): **F A C E** — spells "FACE."
- The clef curls around the **G** line (2nd line up).

## Bass clef (𝄢 F-clef — lower pitches; left hand)
- **Lines** (bottom→top): **G B D F A** — "Good Boys Do Fine Always."
- **Spaces** (bottom→top): **A C E G** — "All Cows Eat Grass."
- The two dots sit around the **F** line (4th line up).

## Middle C
Middle C is **one ledger line below** the treble staff and **one ledger line above** the bass staff — the note that links the two clefs on the grand staff.

**Ledger lines** are short lines added above or below the staff for notes outside its range.
