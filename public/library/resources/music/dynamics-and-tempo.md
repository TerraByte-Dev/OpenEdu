# Dynamics, Tempo & Articulation

Most expression markings are **Italian**.

## Dynamics (volume) — softest → loudest
| Mark | Term | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| pp | pianissimo | very soft |
| p | piano | soft |
| mp | mezzo-piano | medium soft |
| mf | mezzo-forte | medium loud |
| f | forte | loud |
| ff | fortissimo | very loud |

- **crescendo** (cresc., or a < hairpin) = gradually louder; **decrescendo / diminuendo** (>) = gradually softer.

## Tempo (speed) — slow → fast (approx. BPM)
- **Largo** very slow (40–60) · **Adagio** slow (66–76) · **Andante** walking (76–108) · **Moderato** moderate (108–120) · **Allegro** fast (120–168) · **Presto** very fast (168–200).
- **ritardando (rit.)** = gradually slowing · **accelerando (accel.)** = gradually speeding up · **a tempo** = return to the original speed.

## Articulation (how notes are played)
- **Staccato** (dot) — short, detached.
- **Legato** (slur) — smooth, connected.
- **Accent** (>) — emphasize the note.
- **Fermata** (𝄐) — hold longer than written.
