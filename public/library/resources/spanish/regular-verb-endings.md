# Spanish Regular Verb Endings — Present Tense

Drop the infinitive ending (**-ar / -er / -ir**), then add the ending for the subject. Models: **hablar** (to speak), **comer** (to eat), **vivir** (to live).

| Subject | -ar → hablar | -er → comer | -ir → vivir |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | hablo | como | vivo |
| tú | hablas | comes | vives |
| él/ella/usted | habla | come | vive |
| nosotros | hablamos | comemos | vivimos |
| vosotros | habláis | coméis | vivís |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | hablan | comen | viven |

## The bare endings
- **-ar:** -o, -as, -a, -amos, -áis, -an
- **-er:** -o, -es, -e, -emos, -éis, -en
- **-ir:** -o, -es, -e, -**imos**, -**ís**, -en

**Notes:** -er and -ir differ only in the *nosotros* and *vosotros* forms. Subject pronouns are usually dropped because the ending shows the person. The Spanish present covers English "I speak," "I am speaking," and "I do speak."
