# Gas Laws

Variables: P = pressure, V = volume, n = moles, T = **absolute temperature in kelvin** (K = °C + 273.15).

| Law | Relationship | Held constant |
|---|---|---|
| **Boyle's** | P₁V₁ = P₂V₂ | T, n |
| **Charles's** | V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂ | P, n |
| **Gay-Lussac's** | P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂ | V, n |
| **Combined** | P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂ | n |
| **Ideal gas** | PV = nRT | R = 8.314 J/(mol·K) |

## How to read them
- **Boyle:** pressure and volume are *inversely* related — squeeze a gas and its pressure rises.
- **Charles:** volume rises with temperature — heat a balloon and it expands.
- Always convert temperature to **kelvin** before using any gas law.

**Example (Boyle):** 2 L at 1 atm compressed to 1 L → pressure doubles to 2 atm.
