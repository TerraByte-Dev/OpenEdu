# Activity Series of Metals

Metals ranked **most → least reactive** (most easily oxidized / lose electrons). A metal will **displace** any metal listed below it from a compound.

**Most reactive**
Li · K · Ba · Sr · Ca · Na · Mg · Al · Zn · Fe · Ni · Sn · Pb · **(H)** · Cu · Hg · Ag · Pt · Au
**Least reactive**

**How to use it**
- A metal displaces the ion of any **less reactive** metal: Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu (zinc is above copper). The reverse does not happen.
- Metals **above hydrogen (H)** react with acids to release H₂ gas; metals **below H** (Cu, Ag, Au) do not.
- The top metals (Li, K, Na, Ca) react even with cold water.
- Gold and platinum are at the bottom — that's why they resist corrosion.

**Halogen activity** (for nonmetal displacement): F₂ > Cl₂ > Br₂ > I₂. Chlorine displaces bromide and iodide from solution.
