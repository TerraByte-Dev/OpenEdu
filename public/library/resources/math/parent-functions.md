# Parent Functions

A *parent function* is the simplest form of a function family; every other graph in the family is a transformation (shift, stretch, or reflection) of it.

| Function | Rule | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Linear | y = x | straight line through the origin |
| Quadratic | y = x² | U-shaped parabola |
| Cubic | y = x³ | S-curve through the origin |
| Absolute value | y = &#124;x&#124; | V-shape |
| Square root | y = √x | half-parabola (x ≥ 0) |
| Reciprocal | y = 1/x | hyperbola (asymptotes on the axes) |
| Exponential | y = bˣ (b > 1) | rapid growth; passes (0, 1) |
| Logarithmic | y = logₐx | slow growth; passes (1, 0) |

## Transformations of y = f(x)
- f(x) + k shifts UP k; f(x) − k shifts down
- f(x − h) shifts RIGHT h; f(x + h) shifts left
- −f(x) reflects over the x-axis; f(−x) over the y-axis
- a·f(x): vertical stretch (|a| > 1) or compression (|a| < 1)

Exponential and logarithmic functions are inverses of each other, as are quadratic and square-root (for x ≥ 0).
