# The Coordinate Plane

The **coordinate (Cartesian) plane** is formed by two perpendicular number lines:
- **x-axis** — horizontal.
- **y-axis** — vertical.
- **Origin** — where they cross, at **(0, 0)**.

**Ordered pairs.** A point is named **(x, y)**: the *x*-coordinate first (right + / left −), the *y*-coordinate second (up + / down −). Always read across, then up/down. (3, 2) means right 3, up 2.

**The four quadrants** (counterclockwise from top right):
- **I** — (+, +) upper right
- **II** — (−, +) upper left
- **III** — (−, −) lower left
- **IV** — (+, −) lower right

Points **on an axis** are in no quadrant: (4, 0) is on the x-axis; (0, −3) is on the y-axis.

The order matters — (3, 2) and (2, 3) are different points. This plane is the basis for graphing equations, lines, and functions.
