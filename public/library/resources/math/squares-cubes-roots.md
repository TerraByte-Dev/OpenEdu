# Squares, Cubes & Roots

**Perfect squares (n²)**
1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100, 121, 144, 169, 196, 225 (1–15);
256, 289, 324, 361, 400 (16–20); 625 (25²), 900 (30²).

**Perfect cubes (n³)**
1, 8, 27, 64, 125, 216, 343, 512, 729, 1000 (1–10); 1331 (11³), 1728 (12³).

**Powers of 2:** 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024.

**Common irrational approximations**
- √2 ≈ 1.414 · √3 ≈ 1.732 · √5 ≈ 2.236 · √7 ≈ 2.646 · √10 ≈ 3.162
- π ≈ 3.14159 · e ≈ 2.71828 · golden ratio φ ≈ 1.618

**Rules:** √(ab) = √a · √b; (√a)² = a; ⁿ√(aⁿ) = a. A square root has **two** values (±); the radical symbol means the **principal (positive)** root. Recognizing perfect squares speeds up simplifying radicals and the quadratic formula.
