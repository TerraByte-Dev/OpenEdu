# Geometry Formulas

Symbols: s = side, l = length, w = width, b = base, h = height, r = radius, π ≈ 3.14159.

## 2D shapes — area (A) and perimeter (P)
- Square: A = s², P = 4s
- Rectangle: A = l × w, P = 2(l + w)
- Triangle: A = ½ × b × h
- Circle: A = πr², circumference C = 2πr
- Parallelogram: A = b × h
- Trapezoid: A = ½ × (a + b) × h   (a and b are the parallel sides)

## 3D solids — volume (V) and surface area (SA)
- Cube: V = s³, SA = 6s²
- Rectangular prism (box): V = l × w × h
- Sphere: V = (4/3)πr³, SA = 4πr²
- Cylinder: V = πr²h, SA = 2πr² + 2πrh
- Cone: V = (1/3)πr²h
- Pyramid: V = (1/3) × (base area) × h

## Right triangles
- Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c²   (c is the hypotenuse, the side opposite the right angle)

Angles in any triangle sum to 180°; angles in a quadrilateral sum to 360°.
