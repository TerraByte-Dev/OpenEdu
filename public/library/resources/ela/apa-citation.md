# APA Citation Format (7th edition)

APA uses **author–date** citation. The list is titled **References**, alphabetical by author, with a **hanging indent**.

## In-text citation
- Parenthetical: **(Author, Year)** — `(Smith, 2020)`.
- With a quotation, add the page: `(Smith, 2020, p. 42)`.
- Narrative (author in the sentence): `Smith (2020) found …`
- Two authors: `(Smith & Jones, 2020)`. Three or more: `(Smith et al., 2020)`.

## Reference templates
- **Book:** Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of the work: Subtitle* (ed.). Publisher.
  *Morrison, T. (1987). Beloved. Knopf.*
- **Journal article:** Author, A. A. (Year). Title of the article. *Journal Name, Volume*(Issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxx
- **Web page:** Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). *Title of page*. Site Name. URL

## Style rules
- **Sentence case** for article/book titles (capitalize only the first word, the first word after a colon, and proper nouns); **italicize** book and journal titles.
- List up to **20 authors** in a reference; the publisher **location is not** included (a 7th-ed. change).
- Use **"p."/"pp."** for pages in-text, and treat a **DOI as a URL** (`https://doi.org/…`).
