# Greek and Latin Roots

Knowing roots and affixes lets you decode unfamiliar words.

**Common roots** (meaning — example)
- *bio* life — biology; *geo* earth — geography; *photo* light — photograph
- *graph/gram* write/draw — paragraph; *scrib/script* write — describe
- *port* carry — transport; *dict* say — predict; *spect* look — inspect
- *struct* build — construct; *aud* hear — audible; *vis/vid* see — visible
- *tele* far — telephone; *chrono* time — chronology; *therm* heat — thermometer
- *aqua/hydro* water — aquarium/hydrate; *phil* love — philosophy
- *-logy* study of — biology; *bene* good — benefit; *mal* bad — malfunction

**Prefixes:** *un-/in-/non-* not · *re-* again · *pre-* before · *dis-* opposite · *sub-* under · *inter-* between · *trans-* across · *anti-* against · *super-* above · *mis-* wrong.

**Suffixes:** *-tion/-sion* act/state · *-able/-ible* capable of · *-ous* full of · *-ist* one who · *-ment* result · *-ize* make · *-ology* study of.
