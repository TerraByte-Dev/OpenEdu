# Figurative Language

Language that means something beyond its literal words, used for effect.

- **Simile** — compares two things using *like* or *as*: *brave as a lion*.
- **Metaphor** — states one thing **is** another: *time is money*.
- **Personification** — gives human traits to non-human things: *the wind whispered*.
- **Hyperbole** — extreme exaggeration: *I've told you a million times*.
- **Idiom** — a phrase whose meaning isn't literal: *break a leg* (good luck).
- **Onomatopoeia** — a word that imitates a sound: *buzz, splash, clang*.
- **Alliteration** — repeated initial consonant sounds: *Peter Piper picked*.
- **Oxymoron** — two contradictory words together: *bittersweet, deafening silence*.
- **Pun** — a play on words with two meanings.
- **Allusion** — a reference to a known work, person, or event.
- **Metonymy/Synecdoche** — a part or related thing stands for the whole: *the **crown** ruled*; *all hands on deck*.

Simile and metaphor are both **comparisons**; a simile says *like/as*, a metaphor says *is*.
