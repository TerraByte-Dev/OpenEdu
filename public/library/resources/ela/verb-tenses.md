# Verb Tenses

English has **12 tenses** — three times (past, present, future) × four aspects (simple, progressive, perfect, perfect-progressive). Shown with the verb *walk*:

**Present**
- Simple: *I walk* — habits, facts.
- Progressive: *I am walking* — happening now.
- Perfect: *I have walked* — past action linked to now.
- Perfect progressive: *I have been walking* — ongoing up to now.

**Past**
- Simple: *I walked* — finished past action.
- Progressive: *I was walking* — was in progress.
- Perfect: *I had walked* — before another past event.
- Perfect progressive: *I had been walking*.

**Future**
- Simple: *I will walk*.
- Progressive: *I will be walking*.
- Perfect: *I will have walked* — done by a future time.
- Perfect progressive: *I will have been walking*.

Aspect pattern: **progressive** uses *be* + *-ing*; **perfect** uses *have* + past participle; **perfect-progressive** combines both (*have been* + *-ing*).
