# Plot Diagram (Freytag's Pyramid)

A story's plot is the arrangement of events. The classic **Freytag's Pyramid** maps it as five stages that rise to a peak and fall.

1. **Exposition** — the beginning: introduces the setting, main characters, and situation. The **inciting incident** here sets the conflict in motion.
2. **Rising Action** — a series of events and complications that build the central **conflict** and increase tension.
3. **Climax** — the turning point and moment of greatest tension; the protagonist faces the conflict head-on. Everything pivots here.
4. **Falling Action** — events that follow the climax as the conflict begins to unwind; loose ends start to resolve.
5. **Resolution (Dénouement)** — the end: the conflict is settled and the story reaches its final outcome.

## Key terms
- **Conflict** — the central struggle. *Types:* character vs. self, vs. character, vs. nature, vs. society, vs. fate/technology.
- **Inciting incident** — the event that starts the main conflict.
- **Protagonist / antagonist** — the main character / the force opposing them.

Rising action is usually the longest stage; falling action and resolution are often brief.
