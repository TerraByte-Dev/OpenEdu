# The Four Factors of Production

Every good or service is made from four kinds of resources:

| Factor | What it is | Examples | Income earned |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Land** | natural resources | water, soil, oil, minerals, forests | **Rent** |
| **Labor** | human physical & mental effort | workers, skills, time | **Wages** |
| **Capital** | human-made tools used to produce | machines, factories, tools, computers | **Interest** |
| **Entrepreneurship** | organizes the other three and takes the risk | business founders, inventors | **Profit** |

- **Capital** here means *physical* capital (tools/equipment), not money. Money used to buy them is "financial capital."
- The entrepreneur combines land, labor, and capital into a business and is rewarded with profit (or absorbs the loss).
- Because resources are **scarce**, every economy must decide **what** to produce, **how**, and **for whom**.
