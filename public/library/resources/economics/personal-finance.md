# Personal Finance Basics

## Interest
- **Simple interest:** `I = P × r × t` (principal × annual rate × years).
- **Compound interest:** `A = P(1 + r/n)^(n·t)` — interest earns interest, so it grows faster than simple.
- **Rule of 72:** years to **double** your money ≈ `72 ÷ rate(%)`. (At 6%, ~12 years.)

## Budgeting — the 50/30/20 rule
Of your take-home pay: **50% needs** (rent, food, bills) · **30% wants** · **20% savings & debt payoff**.

## Credit
- A **credit score** (FICO, **300–850**) predicts how reliably you repay. Higher = lower interest rates.
- Build it by **paying on time** and keeping balances low; late payments and maxed cards hurt it.

## Accounts & pay
- **Checking** = everyday spending; **savings** = earns interest; keep an **emergency fund** of 3–6 months of expenses.
- **Gross pay** is before taxes/deductions; **net pay** ("take-home") is after.

Start saving early — compound interest rewards time more than amount.
