# Supply and Demand

Prices in a market are set where supply meets demand.

- **Law of demand:** as price **rises**, quantity demanded **falls** (inverse) → the **demand curve slopes down**.
- **Law of supply:** as price **rises**, quantity supplied **rises** (direct) → the **supply curve slopes up**.
- **Equilibrium:** the price and quantity where the two curves cross — supply = demand. The market tends toward it.

## Surplus vs shortage
- **Surplus** (price too **high**): quantity supplied > quantity demanded → unsold goods → price falls toward equilibrium.
- **Shortage** (price too **low**): quantity demanded > quantity supplied → buyers compete → price rises toward equilibrium.

## What shifts the whole curve
- **Demand shifters:** buyers' income, tastes, price of related goods (substitutes/complements), number of buyers, expectations.
- **Supply shifters:** input/resource costs, technology, number of sellers, taxes & subsidies, expectations.

A change in **price** moves you *along* a curve; any **other** factor **shifts** the curve left or right.
