# The Constitutional Amendments

There are **27 amendments**. The first ten, ratified in 1791, are the **Bill of Rights**.

## Bill of Rights (1–10)
1. Freedom of religion, speech, press, assembly, and petition
2. Right to keep and bear arms
3. No forced quartering of soldiers
4. No unreasonable searches or seizures (warrants need probable cause)
5. Due process; no double jeopardy or self-incrimination
6. Speedy, public jury trial; right to a lawyer
7. Jury trial in civil cases
8. No cruel and unusual punishment; no excessive bail
9. Rights not listed are still kept by the people
10. Powers not given to the U.S. belong to the states or the people

## Key later amendments
- **13 (1865)** abolished slavery
- **14 (1868)** citizenship + equal protection
- **15 (1870)** vote regardless of race
- **19 (1920)** women's right to vote
- **22 (1951)** two-term limit for the President
- **26 (1971)** voting age lowered to 18

(11, 12, 16–18, 20–25, 27 cover taxes, elections, Prohibition and its repeal, terms of office, and more.)
