# Civil War & Reconstruction (1861–1877)

The war over slavery and union, and the rebuilding that followed.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Nov 1860 | **Lincoln elected**; Southern states begin to secede |
| Apr 1861 | **Fort Sumter** attacked — the Civil War begins |
| Jan 1863 | **Emancipation Proclamation** frees enslaved people in rebel states |
| Jul 1863 | **Gettysburg** — turning point; Union victory |
| Apr 1865 | **Lee surrenders at Appomattox**; war ends. Lincoln assassinated days later |
| 1865 | **13th Amendment** — abolishes slavery |
| 1868 | **14th Amendment** — citizenship & equal protection |
| 1870 | **15th Amendment** — voting rights regardless of race |
| 1877 | **Compromise of 1877** — federal troops leave the South; Reconstruction ends |

**Civil War: 1861–1865.** **Reconstruction: 1865–1877.** The Union was preserved and slavery ended (~620,000+ died), but after 1877 "Jim Crow" segregation took hold across the South.
