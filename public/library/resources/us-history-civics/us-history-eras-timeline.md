# U.S. History Eras Timeline

- **Exploration & Colonization (1492–1763)** — European contact (1492); Jamestown (1607); the 13 colonies.
- **Revolution & Founding (1763–1789)** — taxes and protest; **Declaration of Independence (1776)**; Constitution ratified (1788).
- **Expansion & Early Republic (1789–1861)** — Louisiana Purchase (1803); Manifest Destiny; westward growth and rising tension over slavery.
- **Civil War & Reconstruction (1861–1877)** — Union vs. Confederacy; slavery abolished; rebuilding the South.
- **Industrialization & Gilded Age (1877–1900)** — railroads, factories, mass immigration, big business.
- **Progressive Era & World Wars (1900–1945)** — reforms; **WWI (1917–18)**; Great Depression (1930s); **WWII (1941–45)**.
- **Cold War (1945–1991)** — containment of the USSR; Civil Rights Movement; Korea and Vietnam.
- **Contemporary Era (1991–present)** — post–Cold War; digital age; War on Terror.

These periods overlap at the edges; dates mark the defining turning points historians use.
