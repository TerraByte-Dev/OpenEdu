# Major U.S. Wars

| War | Years | Opponent(s) | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **American Revolution** | 1775–1783 | Great Britain | U.S. independence (Treaty of Paris) |
| **War of 1812** | 1812–1815 | Great Britain | Stalemate; surge of U.S. nationalism |
| **Mexican–American War** | 1846–1848 | Mexico | U.S. win; gained the Southwest (Mexican Cession) |
| **Civil War** | 1861–1865 | Confederacy | Union preserved; slavery abolished |
| **Spanish–American War** | 1898 | Spain | U.S. win; gained Puerto Rico, Guam, Philippines |
| **World War I** | 1917–1918* | Central Powers | Allied victory |
| **World War II** | 1941–1945* | Axis Powers | Allied victory |
| **Korean War** | 1950–1953 | North Korea, China | Armistice; stalemate at the 38th parallel |
| **Vietnam War** | 1965–1973* | North Vietnam | U.S. withdrawal; South falls (1975) |
| **Persian Gulf War** | 1990–1991 | Iraq | Coalition victory; Kuwait liberated |
| **War in Afghanistan** | 2001–2021 | Taliban, al-Qaeda | U.S. withdrawal in 2021 |
| **Iraq War** | 2003–2011 | Iraq | Regime toppled; U.S. withdrawal in 2011 |

*Dates marked * are **U.S. involvement** (WWI began 1914; WWII began 1939; Vietnam War 1955–1975). For combatants and treaties, see the *Wars & Treaties* lookup.
