# Qualifications & Terms of Federal Office

| Office | Min. age | Citizenship | Term | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Representative** | 25 | 7 years a citizen | **2 years** | Must live in the state; all 435 seats every election |
| **Senator** | 30 | 9 years a citizen | **6 years** | Must live in the state; ~1/3 elected every 2 years |
| **President** | 35 | **natural-born** citizen | **4 years** | 14 years a U.S. resident; **max 2 terms** (22nd Amendment) |
| **Vice President** | 35 | natural-born citizen | 4 years | Same qualifications as President |

- **Federal judges** (incl. Supreme Court): the Constitution sets **no** age, citizenship, or law-degree requirement. They are **appointed by the President** and **confirmed by the Senate**, and serve "during good Behaviour" — effectively **for life**.
- House terms are short (close to the people); Senate terms are long and staggered (stability); judges are unelected (independence).
