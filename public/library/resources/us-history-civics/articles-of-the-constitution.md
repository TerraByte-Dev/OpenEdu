# The Seven Articles of the Constitution

The original 1787 Constitution has **seven articles**. The first three create the branches (separation of powers).

- **Article I — Legislative.** Creates **Congress** (House + Senate); lists its powers (the "enumerated powers").
- **Article II — Executive.** Creates the **President** (and Vice President); powers, election, term.
- **Article III — Judicial.** Creates the **Supreme Court** and lower federal courts; defines treason.
- **Article IV — The States.** Relations among states: **full faith and credit**, privileges and immunities, extradition, admitting new states.
- **Article V — Amendment.** How to **amend** the Constitution (propose by 2/3 of Congress or a convention; ratify by 3/4 of states).
- **Article VI — Supremacy.** Federal law and treaties are the **supreme law of the land**; oaths; national debts honored.
- **Article VII — Ratification.** Approval by **9 of the 13 states** would establish the Constitution.
