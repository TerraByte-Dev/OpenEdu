# Causes of the American Revolution

Britain won the **French and Indian War (1754–1763)** but ran up huge debt, then taxed the colonies to pay for it — without colonial representation in Parliament.

| Year | Event | Why it mattered |
|---|---|---|
| 1763 | **Proclamation of 1763** | Banned settlement west of the Appalachians — colonists felt restricted |
| 1764 | **Sugar Act** | Tax on sugar/molasses to raise revenue |
| 1765 | **Stamp Act** | Direct tax on paper goods → "**No taxation without representation**" |
| 1767 | **Townshend Acts** | Taxes on glass, paper, tea; boycotts grow |
| 1770 | **Boston Massacre** | British troops kill 5 colonists; propaganda spreads |
| 1773 | **Boston Tea Party** | Colonists dump British tea to protest the Tea Act |
| 1774 | **Intolerable Acts** | Punished Boston → **First Continental Congress** meets |
| 1775 | **Lexington & Concord** | First battles — "the shot heard round the world" |
| 1776 | **Declaration of Independence** | Colonies formally break from Britain (July 4) |

Underlying ideas: natural rights, self-government, and anger at taxation and standing armies imposed from afar.
