# The Bill of Rights (First 10 Amendments)

Ratified **December 15, 1791**. The first ten amendments limit the federal government and protect individual liberties.

1. **Freedoms** — religion, speech, press, assembly, and petition.
2. **Arms** — the right to keep and bear arms.
3. **Quartering** — no forcing citizens to house soldiers in peacetime.
4. **Search & seizure** — no unreasonable searches; warrants require **probable cause**.
5. **Rights of the accused** — grand jury, no double jeopardy, no self-incrimination, **due process**; private property taken only with just compensation.
6. **Fair trial** — speedy, public trial by an impartial jury; right to a lawyer and to confront witnesses.
7. **Civil jury** — jury trial in federal civil cases.
8. **Punishment** — no excessive bail or fines; no cruel and unusual punishment.
9. **Unlisted rights** — rights not listed are still retained by the people.
10. **States/people** — powers not given to the federal government are reserved to the states or the people.

The full set of **27 amendments** (including these 10) is on the *Constitutional Amendments* card.
