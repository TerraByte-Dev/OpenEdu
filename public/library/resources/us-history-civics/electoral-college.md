# The Electoral College

The President is chosen not by direct national vote but by **538 electors**.

## The numbers
- **538 total electors** = **435** House members + **100** Senators + **3** for Washington, D.C. (23rd Amendment).
- A candidate needs a **majority — 270** electoral votes — to win.
- Each state's electors = its number of **Representatives + 2 Senators**. Every state has at least 3.

## How it works
- Voters cast ballots in November; they are really choosing their state's slate of electors.
- **Winner-take-all** in 48 states + D.C. — the statewide winner takes all its electors. **Maine and Nebraska** split by congressional district.
- Electors cast their votes in **December**; Congress counts them on **January 6**.
- If no candidate reaches 270, the **House of Representatives** picks the President, each state delegation getting **one vote** (12th Amendment).

A candidate can **win the presidency while losing the national popular vote** (e.g., 1876, 1888, 2000, 2016).
