# Founding Documents Compared

| Document | Year | Purpose | Legacy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Magna Carta** | 1215 | Limit the English king's power | First limits on government; rule of law, due process |
| **Declaration of Independence** | 1776 | Declare independence from Britain | Natural rights; "all men are created equal"; consent of the governed |
| **Articles of Confederation** | 1781 | First U.S. government (a loose league of states) | **Too weak** — no power to tax or regulate trade; replaced |
| **U.S. Constitution** | 1787 (ratified 1788, effective 1789) | Stronger federal government | Supreme law of the land; separation of powers; federalism |

- The **Declaration** (Thomas Jefferson) explained *why* the colonies broke away; it is not law.
- The **Articles** failed because the national government was too weak, prompting the 1787 Constitutional Convention.
- The **Constitution** fixed those weaknesses and remains the world's oldest written national constitution still in use.
