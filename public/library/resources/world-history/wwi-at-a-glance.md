# World War I at a Glance (1914–1918)

**Sides**
- **Allied Powers:** Britain, France, Russia, Italy (from 1915), and the **United States (from 1917)**.
- **Central Powers:** Germany, Austria-Hungary, the Ottoman Empire, Bulgaria.

**Causes — M.A.I.N.**
- **M**ilitarism — arms races and glorification of the military.
- **A**lliances — secret treaties dragged nations into war.
- **I**mperialism — competition for colonies.
- **N**ationalism — ethnic pride and rivalry.

**Spark:** the assassination of Archduke **Franz Ferdinand** of Austria-Hungary in Sarajevo, **June 28, 1914**.

**Features:** trench warfare, machine guns, poison gas, tanks, and airplanes — a brutal stalemate on the Western Front.

**End & aftermath:** armistice on **November 11, 1918**. The **Treaty of Versailles (1919)** blamed Germany and imposed heavy reparations. Roughly **17 million** died. The **League of Nations** was created. Harsh peace terms helped set the stage for WWII.
