# Cold War Timeline (1947–1991)

A 45-year standoff between the **United States** (capitalist, NATO) and the **Soviet Union** (communist, Warsaw Pact) — rivalry without direct war between them, under the threat of nuclear weapons.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 1947 | **Truman Doctrine** & Marshall Plan — U.S. policy of **containment** |
| 1948–49 | **Berlin Airlift** supplies blockaded West Berlin |
| 1949 | **NATO** formed; USSR tests an atomic bomb; Communists win in China |
| 1950–53 | **Korean War** ends in stalemate |
| 1955 | **Warsaw Pact** formed |
| 1961 | **Berlin Wall** built |
| 1962 | **Cuban Missile Crisis** — closest brush with nuclear war |
| 1969 | U.S. **Moon landing** wins the Space Race |
| 1989 | the **Berlin Wall falls** |
| 1991 | the **Soviet Union dissolves** — the Cold War ends |

Proxy conflicts (Korea, Vietnam, Afghanistan) and the arms and space races defined the era.
