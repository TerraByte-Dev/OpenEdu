# World War II at a Glance (1939–1945)

**Sides**
- **Allies:** United States, United Kingdom, Soviet Union, France, China.
- **Axis:** Germany, Italy, Japan.

**Causes:** resentment of the Treaty of Versailles, the **Great Depression**, the rise of **fascism** (Hitler, Mussolini) and Japanese militarism, and Allied **appeasement** of aggression.

**Key dates & turning points**
- **Sept 1, 1939** — Germany invades Poland; war begins in Europe.
- **Dec 7, 1941** — Japan attacks **Pearl Harbor**; the U.S. enters.
- **1942–43** — Midway and Stalingrad turn the tide against the Axis.
- **June 6, 1944** — **D-Day**, the Allied invasion of Normandy.
- **May 8, 1945** — **V-E Day**; Germany surrenders.
- **Aug 6 & 9, 1945** — atomic bombs on **Hiroshima** and **Nagasaki**.
- **Aug/Sept 1945** — Japan surrenders (**V-J Day**).

**Aftermath:** the **Holocaust** murdered ~6 million Jews; **~70–85 million** died overall (deadliest war in history). The **United Nations** was founded (1945); the postwar U.S.–Soviet rivalry soon led into the **Cold War** (from 1947).
