# Major Political Revolutions

**Enlightenment** ideas — natural rights, popular sovereignty, the social contract — fueled an "Age of Revolutions" across the Atlantic world.

| Revolution | Dates | Overthrew | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Glorious Revolution** (England) | 1688 | absolute monarchy | constitutional monarchy; English Bill of Rights (1689) |
| **American Revolution** | 1775–1783 | British colonial rule | an independent U.S. republic |
| **French Revolution** | 1789–1799 | the French monarchy (Louis XVI) | a republic; "Liberty, Equality, Fraternity"; led to Napoleon |
| **Haitian Revolution** | 1791–1804 | French rule & slavery | first successful large slave revolt; first Black republic |
| **Latin American revolutions** | 1808–1826 | Spanish & Portuguese rule | independent nations (led by Bolívar, San Martín) |
| **Russian Revolution** | 1917 | the Tsar (monarchy) | a communist state — the USSR (Lenin, the Bolsheviks) |

Common threads: anger at unequal rule and taxes, Enlightenment ideals, and demands for rights and self-government.
