# Major World Religions

| Religion | Origin / Founder | Began | Sacred text | Core belief | Adherents |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Judaism** | Abraham, Middle East | c. 1800 BCE | Torah (Tanakh) | monotheism; covenant with God | ~15 million |
| **Christianity** | Jesus, Roman Judea | c. 30 CE | Bible | monotheism (Trinity); salvation through Jesus | ~2.4 billion |
| **Islam** | Muhammad, Arabia | c. 610 CE | Qur'an | monotheism; submission to Allah | ~1.9 billion |
| **Hinduism** | no single founder, India | c. 1500 BCE | Vedas, Bhagavad Gita | dharma, karma, reincarnation | ~1.2 billion |
| **Buddhism** | Siddhartha Gautama (Buddha), India | c. 500 BCE | Tripitaka (sutras) | Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, nirvana | ~500 million |

- **Abrahamic** (one God, Middle Eastern roots): Judaism, Christianity, Islam.
- **Dharmic** (Indian origin, karma/rebirth): Hinduism, Buddhism.
- Largest: **Christianity**, then **Islam**. Adherent counts are recent estimates and shift over time.
