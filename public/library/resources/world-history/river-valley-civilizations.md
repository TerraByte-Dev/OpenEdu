# Ancient River-Valley Civilizations

The first cities grew in fertile river valleys, where farming surpluses freed people for other work.

| Civilization | River(s) | Region | Began | Writing | Known for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mesopotamia** | Tigris & Euphrates | Fertile Crescent (Iraq) | c. 3500 BCE | cuneiform | first cities (Sumer), the wheel, Code of Hammurabi (Babylon), ziggurats |
| **Ancient Egypt** | Nile | NE Africa | c. 3100 BCE | hieroglyphics | pharaohs, pyramids, mummification |
| **Indus Valley** (Harappan) | Indus | Pakistan / NW India | c. 2500 BCE | undeciphered script | planned brick cities (Harappa, Mohenjo-Daro), drainage |
| **Ancient China** (Shang) | Huang He (Yellow R.) | East Asia | c. 1600 BCE | oracle-bone script | bronze casting, dynasties, ancestor veneration |

Shared traits: a reliable river for water and silt, surplus food, cities, writing, organized religion, social classes, and a central government.
