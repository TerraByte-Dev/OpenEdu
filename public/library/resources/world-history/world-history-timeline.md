# World History Timeline

(Before writing: the **Stone Age** and the **Agricultural Revolution**, ~10,000 BCE, when farming began.)

- **River-Valley Civilizations (c. 3500–800 BCE)** — Mesopotamia, Egypt, Indus, and China; the first cities and writing.
- **Classical Era (c. 800 BCE–500 CE)** — Greece, Rome, Han China, and Maurya/Gupta India; major religions and philosophies.
- **Postclassical / Middle Ages (c. 500–1500)** — spread of Islam, feudal Europe, Tang/Song China, and trade networks like the Silk Road.
- **Early Modern Era (c. 1500–1800)** — Renaissance, Age of Exploration, the Columbian Exchange, Reformation, Scientific Revolution, and Enlightenment.
- **Modern Era (c. 1800–1945)** — Industrial Revolution, imperialism, and the two World Wars.
- **Contemporary Era (1945–present)** — the Cold War, decolonization, globalization, and the digital age.

Dates are approximate turning points; eras overlap and differ by region.
