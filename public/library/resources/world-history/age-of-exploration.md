# Age of Exploration (c. 1400s–1500s)

Europeans sought sea routes to Asia's spices and riches — driven by "**God, Gold, and Glory**" and enabled by the caravel, compass, and astrolabe.

| Explorer | Sponsor | Date | Achievement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bartolomeu Dias** | Portugal | 1488 | rounded Africa's Cape of Good Hope |
| **Christopher Columbus** | Spain | 1492 | reached the Caribbean (the Americas) |
| **John Cabot** | England | 1497 | explored the North American coast |
| **Vasco da Gama** | Portugal | 1498 | first sea route to India around Africa |
| **Amerigo Vespucci** | Spain/Portugal | 1499–1502 | recognized the Americas as a "New World" (its namesake) |
| **Ferdinand Magellan** | Spain | 1519–1522 | expedition first to **circumnavigate** the globe (Magellan died en route; Elcano finished it) |
| **Hernán Cortés** | Spain | 1519–1521 | conquered the **Aztec** Empire |
| **Francisco Pizarro** | Spain | 1532 | conquered the **Inca** Empire |

Consequences: the **Columbian Exchange**, vast colonial empires, the Atlantic slave trade, and the collapse of Native American populations from disease.
