# Classical Civilizations Compared

The "classical" empires (c. 800 BCE–500 CE) shaped law, government, religion, and science for centuries.

| Civilization | Dates | Government | Known for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Greece** | c. 800–146 BCE | city-states; Athenian **democracy** | philosophy (Socrates, Plato, Aristotle), the Olympics, drama, math |
| **Rome** | 509 BCE–476 CE (West) | **republic → empire** | law, roads & aqueducts, Latin, the spread of Christianity |
| **Han China** | 206 BCE–220 CE | centralized empire; civil-service bureaucracy | the Silk Road, paper, the seismograph, Confucianism |
| **India** (Maurya & Gupta) | Maurya 322–185 BCE; Gupta c. 320–550 CE | empires | a "Golden Age": the concept of **zero**, decimals, the spread of Buddhism & Hinduism |

Each fell to internal decline and outside invasion, but their ideas — democracy, republics, Roman law, Confucian government, Indian mathematics — endured.
