# The Columbian Exchange

After Columbus's **1492** voyage, plants, animals, people, and diseases moved between the **Old World** (Europe, Africa, Asia) and the **New World** (the Americas) — reshaping diets and populations worldwide.

## Old World → New World
- **Animals:** horses, cattle, pigs, sheep, chickens.
- **Crops:** wheat, rice, sugarcane, coffee, bananas, citrus.
- **Diseases:** smallpox, measles, influenza — killed a huge share of Native Americans, who had no immunity.

## New World → Old World
- **Crops:** potatoes, maize (corn), tomatoes, cacao (chocolate), beans, squash, peppers, vanilla, tobacco.
- **Animals:** the turkey.

## Effects
- New foods (potatoes, maize) fueled population growth in Europe, Africa, and Asia.
- Old World diseases devastated Native populations.
- Demand for plantation labor drove the **Atlantic slave trade**.
