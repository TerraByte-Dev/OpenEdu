# Greek Alphabet

The 24 letters of the Greek alphabet. Format: **Name — uppercase lowercase — common use**.

- Alpha — Α α — angles, alpha particle
- Beta — Β β — angles, beta particle
- Gamma — Γ γ — gamma rays, photon
- Delta — Δ δ — change (Δ), small quantity (δ)
- Epsilon — Ε ε — a tiny value
- Zeta — Ζ ζ
- Eta — Η η — efficiency
- Theta — Θ θ — angles
- Iota — Ι ι
- Kappa — Κ κ
- Lambda — Λ λ — wavelength, eigenvalues
- Mu — Μ μ — micro prefix, mean, friction
- Nu — Ν ν — frequency
- Xi — Ξ ξ
- Omicron — Ο ο
- Pi — Π π — ratio ≈ 3.14159, product (Π)
- Rho — Ρ ρ — density
- Sigma — Σ σ — sum (Σ), standard deviation (σ)
- Tau — Τ τ — torque, time constant
- Upsilon — Υ υ
- Phi — Φ φ — golden ratio, angles
- Chi — Χ χ — chi-squared
- Psi — Ψ ψ — wave function
- Omega — Ω ω — ohms (Ω), angular velocity (ω)
