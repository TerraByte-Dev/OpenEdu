# World Political Map

Earth has **7 continents** and about **195 countries** (193 UN members + 2 observer states).

## Continents (by land area, largest → smallest)
Asia · Africa · North America · South America · Antarctica · Europe · Australia/Oceania.

## Reading the map
- The **equator** (0° latitude) splits Earth into the **Northern** and **Southern** hemispheres.
- The **prime meridian** (0° longitude, through Greenwich) splits it into the **Eastern** and **Western** hemispheres.
- A **political** map shows human-made borders (countries, states, capitals); a **physical** map shows natural features (see the world-physical-map card).
- This map uses an **equirectangular** projection (latitude/longitude as a simple grid), which stretches areas near the poles — Greenland looks far larger than it is.

## Most populous countries
India and China (each ~1.4 billion), then the United States, Indonesia, Pakistan, Nigeria, Brazil.

For every country's **capital**, see the **countries-capitals** card and the **country-profiles** lookup; for continent/ocean areas, see **continents-oceans**.
