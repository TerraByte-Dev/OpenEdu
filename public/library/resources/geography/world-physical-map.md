# World Physical Map

A **physical** map shows natural features — mountains, rivers, deserts, plains — rather than borders.

## Major mountain ranges
- **Himalayas** (Asia) — highest on Earth; **Mt. Everest 8,849 m** is the tallest peak.
- **Andes** (South America) — the longest continental range (~7,000 km).
- **Rockies** (North America) · **Alps** (Europe) · **Urals** (Europe–Asia divide) · **Atlas** (NW Africa) · **Great Dividing Range** (Australia).
- Ranges rise mainly where **tectonic plates collide**.

## Major rivers
- **Nile** (Africa) — ~6,650 km, generally called the longest.
- **Amazon** (South America) — the largest by water volume (drains ~1/5 of the world's river flow).
- **Yangtze** (Asia, longest in Asia) · **Mississippi–Missouri** (North America) · **Ganges** (sacred, S. Asia) · **Congo** (Africa) · **Volga** (longest in Europe).

## Major deserts
- **Sahara** (Africa) — the largest hot desert (~9.2 million km²).
- **Arabian** · **Gobi** (Asia) · **Kalahari** (Africa) · **Great Sandy** (Australia) · **Atacama** (the driest non-polar place on Earth).
- The largest desert overall is the cold **Antarctic** ice desert (its Dry Valleys are the driest place of all).
