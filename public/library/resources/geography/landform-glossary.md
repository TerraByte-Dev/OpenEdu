# Landform Glossary

## Land features
- **Mountain** — a high, steep rise of land, much taller than a hill.
- **Hill** — raised land, lower and more rounded than a mountain.
- **Plateau** — a flat, elevated area of land ("tableland").
- **Plain** — flat or gently rolling lowland.
- **Valley** — low land between hills or mountains.
- **Canyon** — a deep, narrow valley with steep sides, cut by a river.
- **Basin** — a low area surrounded by higher land.
- **Desert** — a very dry region with little rainfall.

## Land meeting water
- **Peninsula** — land surrounded by water on **three** sides.
- **Island** — land surrounded by water on **all** sides.
- **Isthmus** — a narrow strip of land joining two larger areas (e.g., Panama).
- **Delta** — a triangular deposit of soil where a river meets the sea.
- **Coast / shore** — where land meets the ocean.

## Water features
- **Strait** — a narrow waterway connecting two larger bodies of water.
- **Bay / Gulf** — water partly enclosed by land (a gulf is larger).
- **Tributary** — a smaller stream that flows into a larger river.
