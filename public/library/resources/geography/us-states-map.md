# United States Map (50 States)

The United States has **50 states** plus the federal district of **Washington, D.C.** The map uses the **Albers USA** projection, which repositions **Alaska** and **Hawaii** as insets so the whole country fits one frame.

## Regions (US Census Bureau)
- **Northeast:** ME, NH, VT, MA, RI, CT, NY, NJ, PA.
- **Midwest:** OH, MI, IN, IL, WI, MN, IA, MO, ND, SD, NE, KS.
- **South:** DE, MD, DC, VA, WV, NC, SC, GA, FL, KY, TN, AL, MS, AR, LA, OK, TX.
- **West:** MT, ID, WY, CO, NM, AZ, UT, NV, WA, OR, CA, AK, HI.

## Useful facts
- Largest by area: **Alaska**; smallest: **Rhode Island**. Most populous: **California**; least: **Wyoming**.
- The two-letter codes are USPS **postal abbreviations** (CA, NY, TX…).
- The 48 connected states are the **contiguous** (or "lower 48") states; with Alaska and Hawaii (admitted 1959) that makes 50.

For each state's **capital, statehood year, nickname, and largest city**, use the **us-states** lookup (e.g. "California state facts").
