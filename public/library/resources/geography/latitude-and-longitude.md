# Latitude and Longitude

Every place on Earth has an address made of two angles.

- **Latitude** — **parallels** running east–west; they measure distance **north or south of the Equator (0°)**, from **0° to 90°**. The Equator is 0°; the poles are 90°N and 90°S.
- **Longitude** — **meridians** running pole to pole; they measure distance **east or west of the Prime Meridian (0°)**, from **0° to 180°**. The Prime Meridian passes through Greenwich, England.

## Key lines
- **Equator** 0° · **Tropic of Cancer** 23.5°N · **Tropic of Capricorn** 23.5°S
- **Arctic Circle** 66.5°N · **Antarctic Circle** 66.5°S
- **Prime Meridian** 0° · **International Date Line** ≈ 180°

## Hemispheres
The **Equator** splits Northern/Southern; the **Prime Meridian** (and 180°) splits Eastern/Western.

## Reading coordinates
Write **latitude, then longitude** — e.g., **(33.7°N, 84.4°W) ≈ Atlanta**. Tip: latitude lines lie *flat* like ladder rungs; longitude lines run top to bottom.
