# Continents and Oceans

Earth has **7 continents** and **5 oceans**.

## The 7 continents (largest to smallest by area)
- Asia — largest continent (~44.6 million km²); most populous (~4.7 billion people).
- Africa — 2nd largest (~30.3 million km²); ~1.4 billion people.
- North America — ~24.7 million km²; includes Canada, USA, Mexico, Central America.
- South America — ~17.8 million km²; home to the Amazon rainforest.
- Antarctica — ~14.2 million km²; ice-covered, coldest, no permanent residents.
- Europe — ~10.2 million km²; densely populated.
- Australia/Oceania — smallest (~8.6 million km²); includes Australia and Pacific islands.

## The 5 oceans (largest to smallest)
- Pacific Ocean — largest and deepest; contains the Mariana Trench (~10,935 m deep).
- Atlantic Ocean — 2nd largest; separates the Americas from Europe and Africa.
- Indian Ocean — 3rd largest; warmest ocean.
- Southern Ocean — surrounds Antarctica (recognized as distinct since 2000).
- Arctic Ocean — smallest and shallowest; mostly ice-covered.

Oceans cover about 71% of Earth's surface; all are connected into one global ocean.
