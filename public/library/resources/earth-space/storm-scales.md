# Storm & Magnitude Scales

**Saffir–Simpson Hurricane Scale** (1-min sustained wind)
- Cat 1: 74–95 mph · Cat 2: 96–110 · Cat 3: 111–129 (major) · Cat 4: 130–156 · Cat 5: ≥157 mph (catastrophic).

**Enhanced Fujita (EF) Tornado Scale** (estimated from damage)
- EF0: 65–85 mph · EF1: 86–110 · EF2: 111–135 · EF3: 136–165 · EF4: 166–200 · EF5: >200 mph.

**Earthquake Magnitude** (moment magnitude / Richter)
- Logarithmic: each whole step = **10× ground shaking** and **~31.6× energy**. <3 barely felt; 5–6 moderate damage; 7+ major; 8+ great. There is no upper limit.

**Beaufort Wind Scale** (observation-based, 0–12)
- 0 calm · 3 gentle breeze · 6 strong breeze · 8 gale · 10 storm · 12 hurricane-force (≥73 mph).

Hurricane and tornado scales rate **wind**; the magnitude scale rates **energy released** by an earthquake (intensity/shaking is separately rated by the Modified Mercalli I–XII scale).
