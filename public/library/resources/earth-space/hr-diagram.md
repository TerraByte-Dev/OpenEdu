# The H-R Diagram

The **Hertzsprung–Russell diagram** plots stars by **luminosity** (brightness, vertical axis) against **surface temperature** (horizontal axis, plotted hot→cool, left→right). Where a star sits reveals its stage of life.

## The main regions
- **Main sequence** — the diagonal band from hot-and-bright (top-left) to cool-and-dim (bottom-right). ~90% of stars, including the **Sun**, fuse hydrogen here.
- **Giants & supergiants** — top-right: cool but very luminous because they are huge.
- **White dwarfs** — bottom-left: hot but dim because they are tiny (dense stellar remnants).

## Star life cycle (Sun-like star)
nebula → protostar → **main sequence** → red giant → planetary nebula → white dwarf.

Very massive stars instead end as a **supernova**, leaving a neutron star or black hole. A star's mass at birth decides its temperature, lifetime, and fate — the more massive, the hotter, brighter, and shorter-lived.
