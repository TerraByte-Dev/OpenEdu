# The Solar System

The 8 planets in order from the Sun (mnemonic: **My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Nachos**):

| Planet | Distance (AU) | Orbit (year) | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mercury | 0.39 | 88 days | terrestrial (rocky) |
| Venus | 0.72 | 225 days | terrestrial |
| Earth | 1.00 | 365 days | terrestrial |
| Mars | 1.52 | 687 days | terrestrial |
| Jupiter | 5.20 | 12 years | gas giant (largest) |
| Saturn | 9.58 | 29 years | gas giant (rings) |
| Uranus | 19.2 | 84 years | ice giant |
| Neptune | 30.1 | 165 years | ice giant (farthest) |

- **1 AU** (astronomical unit) = the Earth–Sun distance ≈ 150 million km.
- The four inner planets are small and rocky; the four outer planets are large and gaseous, separated by the **asteroid belt**.
- **Pluto** is a *dwarf planet* (reclassified in 2006), along with Ceres, Eris, and others.
- The Sun holds ~99.8% of the solar system's mass; planets orbit in the same direction in nearly the same plane.
