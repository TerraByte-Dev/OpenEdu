# Layers of the Atmosphere

Five layers, lowest to highest. (Boundaries vary with latitude and season; values are typical.)

- **Troposphere** — 0 to ~12 km. Where we live; holds most of the air mass and nearly all **weather** and water vapor. Temperature *falls* with altitude.
- **Stratosphere** — ~12 to 50 km. Contains the **ozone layer**, which absorbs UV; temperature *rises* with altitude. Jets cruise in the lower stratosphere.
- **Mesosphere** — ~50 to 85 km. Coldest layer (down to about −90 °C); most **meteors burn up** here.
- **Thermosphere** — ~85 to 600 km. Very thin air; absorbs high-energy solar radiation, so it's extremely hot. Home to **auroras** and the ISS.
- **Exosphere** — ~600 to 10,000 km. The outermost layer; air fades into space.

The boundary between layers is a "-pause": tropopause, stratopause, mesopause. The **ionosphere** (charged particles that reflect radio waves) overlaps the meso/thermosphere.
