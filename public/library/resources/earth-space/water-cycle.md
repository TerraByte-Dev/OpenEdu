# The Water Cycle

The **hydrologic cycle** is the continuous movement of water between the oceans, atmosphere, and land. The **Sun's energy** and **gravity** drive it. Water is never created or destroyed — only moved and changed in state.

**Main processes**
- **Evaporation** — the Sun heats liquid water (oceans, lakes) into water vapor (gas).
- **Transpiration** — plants release water vapor from their leaves. (Evaporation + transpiration = *evapotranspiration*.)
- **Condensation** — rising vapor cools and forms tiny droplets, making **clouds**.
- **Precipitation** — droplets combine and fall as rain, snow, sleet, or hail.
- **Collection / runoff** — water gathers in oceans, lakes, and rivers; surface **runoff** flows downhill.
- **Infiltration** — water soaks into the soil, recharging **groundwater** and aquifers.

Water then evaporates again, repeating the cycle. About 97% of Earth's water is in the oceans; the cycle constantly redistributes the small freshwater fraction.
