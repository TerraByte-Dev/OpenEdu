# The Geologic Time Scale

Earth's 4.6-billion-year history, divided by major changes in life and rock. "Ma" = millions of years ago.

**Eons**
- **Hadean** (4,600–4,000 Ma) — molten early Earth; Moon-forming impact.
- **Archean** (4,000–2,500 Ma) — first life (single-celled bacteria).
- **Proterozoic** (2,500–541 Ma) — oxygen builds up; first eukaryotes.
- **Phanerozoic** (541 Ma–today) — abundant complex life; split into three eras:

**Eras of the Phanerozoic**
- **Paleozoic** (541–252 Ma) — "ancient life": Cambrian explosion, first fish, plants, amphibians, reptiles. Ends with the **Permian extinction** (the "Great Dying," ~90% of all species — up to ~96% of *marine* species).
- **Mesozoic** (252–66 Ma) — "age of reptiles": **dinosaurs**, first birds and mammals. Ends with the **K–Pg extinction** (asteroid wipes out non-bird dinosaurs).
- **Cenozoic** (66 Ma–today) — "age of mammals": rise of mammals, birds, and finally humans (*Homo sapiens* ~300,000 years ago).

Order: Eon → Era → Period → Epoch. Boundaries are set by fossil and rock evidence.
