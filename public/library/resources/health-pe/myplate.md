# MyPlate Food Groups

**MyPlate** (USDA, 2011 — it replaced the Food Pyramid) shows a balanced meal as a divided plate plus a side of dairy.

## The five food groups
- **Vegetables** — the largest section; e.g. broccoli, carrots, spinach, peppers.
- **Grains** — the other large section; make **half whole grains** (brown rice, oats, whole-wheat bread).
- **Fruits** — apples, berries, oranges, melon.
- **Protein** — chicken, fish, eggs, beans, nuts, tofu.
- **Dairy** — milk, yogurt, cheese (or fortified soy); shown as a cup beside the plate.

## Key messages
- **Make half your plate fruits and vegetables.**
- **Make half your grains whole grains.**
- **Vary your protein**, and choose **low-fat or fat-free dairy.**
- **Limit** added sugars, saturated fat, and sodium; drink water instead of sugary drinks.

By area, the order is **vegetables (~40%) > grains (~30%) > protein (~20%) > fruits (~10%)**, with dairy as a cup to the side. Fruits + vegetables fill one half of the plate; grains + protein fill the other half. Balancing the groups gives the body the nutrients it needs.
