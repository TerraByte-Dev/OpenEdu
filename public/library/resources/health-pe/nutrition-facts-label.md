# Nutrition Facts Label

The FDA **Nutrition Facts** label (updated 2016) tells you what's in a packaged food. Read it top to bottom.

## What to check first
- **Serving size** — all the numbers below are **per serving**, not per package.
- **Servings per container** — how many servings the package holds (eat 2 servings = double everything).
- **Calories** — energy per serving (now in large, bold type).

## Nutrients (per serving)
- **Get less of these:** Total Fat (incl. **Saturated** and **Trans** fat), **Cholesterol**, **Sodium**, **Added Sugars**.
- **Get more of these** (the FDA's five under-consumed nutrients): Dietary **Fiber**, **Vitamin D**, **Calcium**, **Iron**, **Potassium**.
- **Also listed: Protein** (shown in grams; it usually carries no %DV unless a protein claim is made or the food is for young children).

## % Daily Value (%DV)
- The **%DV** shows how much one serving contributes to a day's needs, based on a **2,000-calorie** diet.
- Quick rule: **5% DV or less = low; 20% DV or more = high.**
- Use it to compare products and to keep "limit" nutrients low and "get enough" nutrients high.
