# Fitness Components & Target Heart Rate

## The 5 health-related fitness components
1. **Cardiovascular (aerobic) endurance** — heart & lungs supplying oxygen during sustained activity (running, swimming).
2. **Muscular strength** — the most force a muscle can exert at once.
3. **Muscular endurance** — repeating/holding a muscle effort over time.
4. **Flexibility** — range of motion at a joint (stretching).
5. **Body composition** — the ratio of fat to lean mass.

*(Skill-related: agility, balance, coordination, power, reaction time, speed.)*

## Target heart rate
- **Maximum heart rate (MHR) ≈ 220 − age** (beats per minute).
- **Target zone = 50–85% of MHR.** Moderate = 50–70%; vigorous = 70–85%.
- Example (age 15): MHR ≈ 205; target zone ≈ **103–174 bpm.**

## The FITT principle (how to plan exercise)
- **F**requency — how often · **I**ntensity — how hard · **T**ime — how long · **T**ype — what activity.
- Guideline: teens should get **60 minutes** of moderate-to-vigorous activity most days.
