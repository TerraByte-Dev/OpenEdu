# Elements & Principles of Art

The **elements** are the building blocks an artist uses; the **principles** are the ways those elements are arranged.

## The 7 Elements of Art
- **Line** — a path between two points (straight, curved, thick, thin).
- **Shape** — a flat, enclosed 2-D area (geometric or organic).
- **Form** — a 3-D shape with volume (height, width, depth).
- **Space** — the area around/between/within objects (positive & negative; depth).
- **Value** — the lightness or darkness of a tone.
- **Color** — hue, value, and intensity (see the color-wheel card).
- **Texture** — how a surface feels or looks (rough, smooth).

## The Principles of Design
- **Balance** — visual weight is evenly distributed (symmetrical, asymmetrical, radial).
- **Contrast** — differences that create interest (light/dark, big/small).
- **Emphasis** — a focal point that draws the eye.
- **Movement** — the path the eye travels through the work.
- **Pattern / Rhythm** — repeated elements creating a beat.
- **Proportion / Scale** — size relationships between parts.
- **Unity / Harmony** — all parts feel like they belong together.
