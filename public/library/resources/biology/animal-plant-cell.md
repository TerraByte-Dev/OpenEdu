# Animal vs Plant Cell

Both are **eukaryotic** (they have a membrane-bound nucleus). Their shared organelles:

- **Nucleus** — holds DNA; controls the cell. Contains the **nucleolus** (makes ribosomes).
- **Cell membrane** — controls what enters and leaves.
- **Cytoplasm** — jelly-like fluid where organelles sit.
- **Mitochondrion** — releases energy via respiration ("powerhouse").
- **Ribosomes** — build proteins.
- **Endoplasmic reticulum (ER)** — transports materials (rough ER has ribosomes).
- **Golgi apparatus** — packages and ships proteins.

## Plant cells also have
- **Cell wall** — rigid cellulose layer outside the membrane, for support.
- **Chloroplasts** — contain chlorophyll; carry out photosynthesis.
- **Large central vacuole** — stores water and keeps the cell firm.

## Animal cells have
- **Lysosomes** — digest waste and worn-out parts.
- A rounder shape and centrioles for cell division.

So: plant cells are boxy with a wall, chloroplasts, and one big vacuole; animal cells are rounder with lysosomes and no wall.
