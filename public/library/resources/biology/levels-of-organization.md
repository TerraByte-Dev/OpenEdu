# Levels of Biological Organization

Life is organized as a nested hierarchy. Each level is built from the one below it.

1. **Atom** — basic unit of matter (carbon, oxygen).
2. **Molecule** — atoms bonded together (water, DNA, glucose).
3. **Organelle** — a structure inside a cell (nucleus, mitochondrion).
4. **Cell** — the smallest unit of life (a neuron, a muscle cell).
5. **Tissue** — a group of similar cells (muscle tissue).
6. **Organ** — tissues working together (the heart).
7. **Organ system** — organs with a shared job (the circulatory system).
8. **Organism** — a complete individual (one wolf).

The next levels are **ecological**:

9. **Population** — same-species organisms in one area (a wolf pack).
10. **Community** — all populations interacting in an area (wolves, deer, plants).
11. **Ecosystem** — a community plus its nonliving environment (soil, water, air).
12. **Biosphere** — all of Earth's ecosystems combined.

Levels 1–8 go from chemical to whole organism; 9–12 scale up to all life on Earth.
