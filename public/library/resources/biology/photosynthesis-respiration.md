# Photosynthesis vs Cellular Respiration

These are mirror-image processes that cycle carbon and energy between organisms and the air.

## Photosynthesis (stores energy)
**6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂**
- Happens in **chloroplasts** (plants, algae, some bacteria).
- Captures light energy and stores it in glucose; releases oxygen.

## Cellular respiration (releases energy)
**C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂ → 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + energy (ATP)**
- Happens mainly in **mitochondria** (nearly all living cells).
- Breaks glucose down to release usable energy (ATP).

## The connection
The **products of one are the reactants of the other**. Photosynthesis takes in CO₂ and water and gives off O₂; respiration takes in O₂ and glucose and gives off CO₂ and water. Together they form a continuous cycle that powers most life on Earth.
