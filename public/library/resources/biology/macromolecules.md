# Biological Macromolecules

The **four classes** of large carbon-based molecules in living things. Three are polymers built from repeating monomers.

| Class | Monomer | Elements | Function | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carbohydrates | monosaccharide | C, H, O | quick energy; structure | glucose, starch, cellulose |
| Lipids | (glycerol + fatty acids) | C, H, O | energy storage; membranes; insulation | fats, oils, phospholipids, steroids |
| Proteins | amino acid | C, H, O, N (S) | enzymes, structure, transport, signaling | enzymes, hemoglobin, antibodies |
| Nucleic acids | nucleotide | C, H, O, N, P | store & transmit genetic information | DNA, RNA |

**Key points**
- Monomers link by **dehydration synthesis** (remove water) and break by **hydrolysis** (add water).
- Carbs ratio ≈ 1:2:1 (CH₂O). Lipids are **nonpolar** (hydrophobic), so they don't dissolve in water — unlike the other three they are not true polymers.
- A nucleotide = sugar + phosphate + nitrogen base.
