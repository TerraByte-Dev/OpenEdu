# Electronic Component Symbols

The standard (US/ANSI) symbols used in circuit diagrams.

- **Wire / conductor** — a line; a **dot** where wires cross means they are connected (a junction/node).
- **Resistor** — a zigzag; limits current. Unit: ohm (Ω).
- **Capacitor** — two parallel lines; stores charge in an electric field. Unit: farad (F).
- **Battery / cell** — a long line (**+**) and a short line (**−**); a voltage (EMF) source.
- **Ground** — stacked shrinking lines; the common 0 V reference.
- **Switch** — a hinged line; opens or closes the circuit.
- **Diode** — a triangle pointing to a bar; lets current flow **one way** only (arrow = direction).
- **LED** — a diode with two out-arrows; emits light when conducting.
- **Inductor** — a series of coils/loops; stores energy in a magnetic field. Unit: henry (H).
- **Lamp** — a circle with an ✕; a light or resistive load.
- **Fuse** — a line through a box; breaks the circuit if the current is too high.
- **Voltmeter (V)** — measures voltage; connected in **parallel**. **Ammeter (A)** — measures current; connected in **series**.
