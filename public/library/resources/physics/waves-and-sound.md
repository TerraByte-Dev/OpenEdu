# Waves & Sound

A **wave** transfers **energy** without transferring matter.

- **Transverse:** the medium vibrates **⊥** to the wave's motion (light, water ripples, a shaken rope).
- **Longitudinal:** the medium vibrates **∥** to the motion, in compressions and rarefactions (**sound**).

## Parts & equations
- **Amplitude (A):** max displacement → louder sound / brighter light.
- **Wavelength (λ):** crest to crest (or compression to compression).
- **Frequency (f):** cycles per second, in **hertz (Hz)**; **period T = 1/f**.
- **Wave speed:**  **v = f·λ**

## Sound
- Needs a **medium** (no sound in a vacuum). Speed in air ≈ **343 m/s at 20 °C**; faster in liquids and solids.
- **Pitch** ↔ frequency; **loudness** ↔ amplitude (measured in decibels, dB).
- **Doppler effect:** relative motion shifts the heard frequency — approaching → higher pitch, receding → lower.

Light and other electromagnetic waves travel at **c ≈ 3×10⁸ m/s** in a vacuum and need **no** medium (see *Electromagnetic Spectrum*).
