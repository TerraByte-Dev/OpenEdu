# The Electromagnetic Spectrum

All electromagnetic (EM) waves travel at the **speed of light** c ≈ 3×10⁸ m/s in a vacuum, related by **c = λf** (wavelength × frequency). Higher frequency = shorter wavelength = higher energy.

From **longest wavelength / lowest energy** to shortest / highest:

| Band | Wavelength | Everyday use |
|---|---|---|
| Radio | > 1 m | broadcast, Wi-Fi, TV |
| Microwave | 1 m – 1 mm | ovens, radar, phones |
| Infrared | 1 mm – 700 nm | heat, remotes, thermal imaging |
| Visible | 700 – 400 nm | the light our eyes see |
| Ultraviolet | 400 – 10 nm | sunburn, sterilization |
| X-ray | 10 – 0.01 nm | medical imaging |
| Gamma | < 0.01 nm | nuclear, cancer treatment |

## Visible light (ROYGBIV)
Red (~700 nm) → orange → yellow → green → blue → indigo → violet (~400 nm). Red has the longest wavelength and lowest energy; violet the shortest and highest.
