# Index of Refraction

The **refractive index** n tells how much light **slows** in a medium:

  **n = c / v**   (c = 3.00×10⁸ m/s in vacuum; n ≥ 1, no units)

Higher n → slower light → "optically denser," and more bending.

| Medium | n |
|---|---|
| Vacuum | 1.0000 |
| Air | 1.0003 |
| Water (20 °C) | 1.33 |
| Ethanol | 1.36 |
| Glass (crown) | 1.52 |
| Diamond | 2.42 |

## Snell's law
  **n₁·sin θ₁ = n₂·sin θ₂**

Light bends **toward** the normal entering a higher-n medium, and **away** when leaving.

## Critical angle & total internal reflection
Going from dense to less dense (n₁ > n₂), at the **critical angle θ_c** the light refracts along the surface:

  **sin θ_c = n₂ / n₁**

Beyond θ_c, all the light **totally internally reflects** — the basis of fiber optics and the sparkle of diamond (its tiny θ_c ≈ 24°).
