# Projectile Motion

A projectile's **horizontal and vertical motions are independent**, linked only by the time **t**. The path is a **parabola**. (Ignoring air resistance; a = g ≈ 9.81 m/s² downward.)

## The two directions
- **Horizontal (x):** constant velocity — no horizontal force.
  - vₓ = v·cos θ  (constant);  x = vₓ·t
- **Vertical (y):** free fall under gravity.
  - v_y = v·sin θ − g·t;  y = v·sin θ·t − ½·g·t²

## Key results (lands at launch height)
- **Time of flight:**  t = 2·v·sin θ / g
- **Max height:**  H = (v·sin θ)² / (2g)
- **Range:**  R = v²·sin(2θ) / g  → **maximum at θ = 45°**

A ball **dropped** and one **thrown horizontally** from the same height hit the ground **at the same time** — gravity affects the vertical motion identically.
