# Vectors & Trigonometry for Physics

- **Vector:** has magnitude **and** direction — force, velocity, acceleration, displacement.
- **Scalar:** magnitude only — mass, time, speed, energy, temperature.

## Resolving into components
With angle θ measured from the +x-axis:

  **Vₓ = |V|·cos θ**   (horizontal)
  **V_y = |V|·sin θ**   (vertical)

## Recombining
  **|V| = √(Vₓ² + V_y²)**   **θ = tan⁻¹(V_y / Vₓ)**

**SOH-CAH-TOA:** sin θ = opp/hyp · cos θ = adj/hyp · tan θ = opp/adj.

## Adding vectors
Add **component by component**: Rₓ = ΣVₓ, R_y = ΣV_y, then recombine. Graphically, place them **tip-to-tail**.

**Example:** a 10 N force at 30° above horizontal → Vₓ = 10·cos 30° ≈ 8.66 N, V_y = 10·sin 30° = 5.0 N.
