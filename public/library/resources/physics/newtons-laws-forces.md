# Newton's Laws & Forces

## The three laws of motion
1. **Inertia:** an object stays at rest, or moves at constant velocity, unless acted on by a net (unbalanced) force.
2. **F = m·a:** the net force on an object equals its mass times its acceleration. Force is in **newtons** (N); 1 N = 1 kg·m/s².
3. **Action–reaction:** for every force, there is an equal and opposite force on the other object.

## Common force equations
- **Weight:** W = m·g  (g ≈ 9.81 m/s² near Earth)
- **Friction:** f = μ·N  (μ = coefficient of friction, N = normal force)
- **Spring (Hooke's law):** F = k·x
- **Centripetal:** F = m·v² / r  (toward the center of a circle)

A net force of zero means **equilibrium** — the object's velocity does not change. Mass measures inertia (kg); weight is the force of gravity on that mass (N) and changes with location.
