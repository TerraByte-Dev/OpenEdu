# Physics Constants

Format: **name — symbol = value (units)**.

- Speed of light in vacuum — c = 2.998 × 10^8 m/s
- Standard gravity (Earth surface) — g = 9.81 m/s²
- Gravitational constant — G = 6.674 × 10^-11 N·m²/kg²
- Planck constant — h = 6.626 × 10^-34 J·s
- Elementary charge — e = 1.602 × 10^-19 C
- Electron mass — m_e = 9.109 × 10^-31 kg
- Proton mass — m_p = 1.673 × 10^-27 kg
- Boltzmann constant — k_B = 1.381 × 10^-23 J/K
- Avogadro number — N_A = 6.022 × 10^23 /mol
- Molar gas constant — R = 8.314 J/(mol·K)
- Coulomb constant — k = 8.988 × 10^9 N·m²/C²
- Vacuum permittivity — ε0 = 8.854 × 10^-12 F/m
- Vacuum permeability — µ0 = 1.257 × 10^-6 T·m/A
- Atomic mass unit — u = 1.661 × 10^-27 kg

Note: g is the acceleration of gravity at Earth's surface; G is the universal constant in Newton's law of gravitation.
