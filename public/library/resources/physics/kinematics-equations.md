# Kinematics Equations (1-D Motion)

For motion with **constant acceleration**. Variables: u = initial velocity, v = final velocity, a = acceleration, s = displacement, t = time.

1. **v = u + a·t**  (no s)
2. **s = u·t + ½·a·t²**  (no v)
3. **v² = u² + 2·a·s**  (no t)
4. **s = ½·(u + v)·t**  (no a)

Each equation leaves out one variable — choose the one that omits the quantity you neither know nor want.

## Units & sign convention
SI units: displacement m, velocity m/s, acceleration m/s², time s. Pick a positive direction; quantities opposite to it are negative.

## Free fall
Near Earth's surface a = g ≈ 9.81 m/s² downward. A dropped object (u = 0) falls s = ½gt² and reaches v = gt.

**Example:** a car starts at rest (u = 0) and accelerates at 3 m/s² for 4 s: v = 0 + 3·4 = 12 m/s; s = ½·3·4² = 24 m.
