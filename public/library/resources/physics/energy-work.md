# Energy & Work

Energy and work are measured in **joules** (J); power in **watts** (W = J/s).

- **Work:** W = F·d·cosθ  (force × distance moved in the force's direction)
- **Kinetic energy:** KE = ½·m·v²  (energy of motion)
- **Gravitational PE:** PE = m·g·h  (energy of height)
- **Power:** P = W / t = F·v  (rate of doing work)
- **Work–energy theorem:** W_net = ΔKE

## Conservation of energy
Energy is never created or destroyed, only transferred or transformed. With no friction, the **total mechanical energy** KE + PE stays constant — a falling object trades PE for KE.

**Example:** a 2 kg ball dropped from 5 m has PE = 2·9.81·5 ≈ 98 J at the top, all converted to KE just before impact, giving v = √(2·9.81·5) ≈ 9.9 m/s.
