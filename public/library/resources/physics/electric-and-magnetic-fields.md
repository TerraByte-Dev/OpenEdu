# Electric & Magnetic Fields

## Electric
- **Coulomb's law:**  F = k·q₁·q₂ / r²   (k = 8.99×10⁹ N·m²/C², charge q in coulombs C)
  - Like charges **repel**, opposite charges **attract**.
- **Electric field:**  E = F / q = k·Q / r²   (N/C) — points **away** from a + charge, **toward** a − charge.
- **Force on a charge:**  F = q·E
- **Work / energy:**  W = q·V   (V = voltage / potential difference)

## Magnetic
- **Force on a moving charge:**  F = q·v·B·sin θ
- **Force on a current-carrying wire:**  F = B·I·L·sin θ
- **B** = magnetic field strength, in **tesla (T)**.
- A charge feels a magnetic force only when it is **moving** and not parallel to **B**; the direction is given by the **right-hand rule**.

## Electromagnetism
Moving charges (currents) **create** magnetic fields, and a **changing** magnetic field **induces** a voltage (Faraday's law) — the basis of motors, generators, and transformers.
