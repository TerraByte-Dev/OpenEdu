# French Regular Verb Endings — Present Tense

Drop the infinitive ending, then add the present ending. Models: **parler** (-er, to speak), **finir** (-ir, to finish), **vendre** (-re, to sell). **-er verbs are ~90% of all French verbs.**

| Subject | -er → parler | -ir → finir | -re → vendre |
|---|---|---|---|
| je | parle | finis | vends |
| tu | parles | finis | vends |
| il/elle/on | parle | finit | vend |
| nous | parlons | finissons | vendons |
| vous | parlez | finissez | vendez |
| ils/elles | parlent | finissent | vendent |

## The bare endings
- **-er:** -e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent
- **-ir:** -is, -is, -it, -**iss**ons, -**iss**ez, -**iss**ent
- **-re:** -s, -s, —, -ons, -ez, -ent

**Notes:** the **-e, -es, -ent** endings of -er verbs are all **silent** — *je parle, tu parles, ils parlent* sound identical. Before a vowel, **je → j'** (*j'aime*). The French present covers "I speak," "I am speaking," and "I do speak."
