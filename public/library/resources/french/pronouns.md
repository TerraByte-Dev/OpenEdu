# French Pronouns

| Person | Subject | Direct obj. | Indirect obj. | Reflexive | Stressed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1s | je | me | me | me | moi |
| 2s | tu | te | te | te | toi |
| 3s | il/elle/on | le / la | lui | se | lui / elle |
| 1pl | nous | nous | nous | nous | nous |
| 2pl | vous | vous | vous | vous | vous |
| 3pl | ils/elles | les | leur | se | eux / elles |

## How they work
- **Subject** pronouns are required (French rarely drops them). **on** = "one/we" and takes 3rd-person singular.
- **Direct object** = receives the action (*Je le vois* = I see him/it); *le/la → l'* before a vowel.
- **Indirect object** = to/for a person (*Je lui parle* = I speak to him/her).
- **Reflexive** goes with reflexive verbs (*se laver* → *je me lave*).
- **Stressed (disjunctive)** pronouns stand alone, after prepositions, or for emphasis: *avec moi, Moi, je pense que…*

## Placement & order
- Object pronouns go **before the verb**: *Je te le donne.*
- Order before the verb: **me/te/se/nous/vous → le/la/les → lui/leur → y → en.**
- **tu** = informal you; **vous** = formal **or** plural you. *me/te → m'/t'* before a vowel.
