# French Articles & Noun Gender

Every French noun is **masculine or feminine**, and the article shows gender + number. French has a third set — the **partitive** ("some").

## The three article sets
| | masc. sing. | fem. sing. | before vowel/h | plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **definite** (the) | le | la | l' | les |
| **indefinite** (a/an, some) | un | une | — | des |
| **partitive** (some) | du | de la | de l' | des |

*le livre, la table, l'ami, les livres; un chien, une idée, des amis; du pain, de la salade, de l'eau.*

## Telling gender from the ending
- Often **masculine:** -age, -ment, -eau, -isme, -ier (le fromage, le moment).
- Often **feminine:** -tion, -sion, -té, -ette, -ence, -ille (la nation, la liberté).
- These are tendencies with exceptions — **learn each noun with its article.**

## Key rules
- After a **negative**, indefinite/partitive articles become **de / d'**: *Je mange une pomme → Je ne mange pas **de** pomme.*
- **de + le → du**, **de + les → des**; **à + le → au**, **à + les → aux**.
