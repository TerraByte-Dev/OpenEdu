# Map and Compass

A map tells you what is there. A compass tells you which way you are facing. Navigation is the
habit of checking one against the other before you have to.

## Compass bearings

A bearing is an angle measured clockwise from north, in degrees. North is 0, and a full turn is 360.
Every named point on the compass rose is a fixed number of degrees, so a bearing can always be said
two ways — "north-east" and "045" mean the same thing.

| Point | Bearing |
|---|---|
| North | 0 |
| North-north-east | 22 |
| North-east | 45 |
| East-north-east | 67 |
| East | 90 |
| East-south-east | 112 |
| South-east | 135 |
| South-south-east | 157 |
| South | 180 |
| South-west | 225 |
| West | 270 |
| North-west | 315 |

## Map scale

Scale is a ratio: one unit on the map equals that many units on the ground. At 1:25,000, one
centimetre is 25,000 centimetres — 250 metres. Divide the scale by 100,000 to get kilometres per
centimetre, or memorise the common ones.

| Scale | Ground distance per cm |
|---|---|
| 1:10,000 | 100 m |
| 1:25,000 | 250 m |
| 1:40,000 | 400 m |
| 1:50,000 | 500 m |
| 1:63,360 | 634 m |
| 1:100,000 | 1000 m |
| 1:125,000 | 1250 m |
| 1:250,000 | 2500 m |
| 1:500,000 | 5000 m |

## Terrain features

Contour lines join points of equal height. Their shape is the terrain, and each shape has a name you
can say out loud to someone who cannot see your map.

- **Spur** — a finger of high ground running down from a hill, contours pointing away from the peak
- **Re-entrant** — a narrow valley cut into a slope, contours pointing back toward the peak
- **Saddle** — the low point on a ridge between two summits
- **Col** — the pass through a saddle, the usual crossing place
- **Escarpment** — a long steep face where contours crowd on one side and spread on the other
- **Plateau** — high ground that is flat on top, contours widely spaced inside a tight ring
- **Depression** — ground lower than everything around it, marked with inward-pointing ticks
- **Knoll** — a small isolated rise, a single closed contour ring
- **Draw** — a shallow re-entrant with no defined stream
- **Cliff** — contours so close they merge into one line
